    • How to use QuickNotes

    QuickNotes is a simple note-taking app that allows you to quickly jot down your ideas and keep track of them. It helps you stay organized and ensures that you never forget important thoughts or tasks.

    1. Open the app and click on the "Add Note" button.
    2. Type your note in the input field that appears.
    3. Click "Add Note" to save your note.

    Keyboard Shortcuts

    Here are some useful keyboard shortcuts for QuickNotes:

    Shortcut Action
    Ctrl + N Add a new note
    Ctrl + S Save the current note
    Ctrl + F Search notes
